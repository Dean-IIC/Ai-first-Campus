---
title: AI and quantum at HKBK
summary: Two commitments for the next decade. Every engineer learns to build with AI, and the campus prepares students and systems for quantum computing.
status: draft
---

**AI First** means AI is part of how every programme is taught and how campus services work, with clear rules for privacy, fairness and human judgement.

**Quantum Ready** has two halves. Students learn quantum computing hands-on, from simulators to real quantum hardware. And the systems that hold student and research records move to the new quantum-safe encryption standards, so that data stolen today cannot be decrypted by quantum computers later.
