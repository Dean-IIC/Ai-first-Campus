## About IQAC

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) was set up to make quality a continuous part of college life, in line with the guidelines of the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). It works with every department to plan improvements, track progress and share good practice.

## Objectives

- Build a culture of quality in teaching, learning, research and administration.
- Develop clear benchmarks and track performance against them.
- Bring student, parent, alumni and employer feedback into planning.
- Prepare the college for assessment and accreditation.

## Functions

- Collects and analyses feedback from students, faculty, parents, alumni and employers.
- Organises faculty development programmes, workshops and seminars on quality.
- Documents academic and administrative audits and best practices.
- Prepares the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) and other submissions to NAAC.
