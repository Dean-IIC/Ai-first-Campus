## What we take on

- **AI and software**: prototypes, data analysis, machine learning models and web applications.
- **Electronics and IoT**: embedded prototypes, sensor networks and testing.
- **Civil engineering**: structural assessment, materials testing and design review.
- **Mechanical engineering**: design, analysis and manufacturing support.
- **Management**: market studies and business analytics from our MBA faculty.

## How an engagement works

1. **Tell us the problem** in the [Request consultancy](#enquire) tab.
2. **Scoping call.** A faculty coordinator discusses requirements, data and timelines with you.
3. **Proposal.** You receive the scope, team, timeline and fee in writing.
4. **Delivery.** A faculty-led team, often with student researchers, does the work and reports progress.
5. **Handover.** You receive the results, a report and any code or drawings agreed in the proposal.
