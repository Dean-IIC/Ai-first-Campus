## Research centre

HKBK has six research centres for doctoral research: Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics. Scholars work with faculty supervisors and have access to the college's labs, library and computing resources. See all [research centres](/research/centres).

## Focus areas

- **Artificial intelligence and machine learning**: language models, computer vision, AI for education and healthcare.
- **Quantum computing and quantum-safe security**: quantum algorithms, post-quantum cryptography and migration.
- **Electronics**: VLSI, embedded systems, IoT and signal processing.
- **Basic sciences**: materials, photonics, green chemistry, applied mathematics and statistics.
- **Mechanical systems**: design, manufacturing, thermal engineering and robotics.

## Work with us

Companies and research organisations can sponsor projects, co-supervise scholars or share equipment. Start with a [consultancy request](/consultancy#enquire) or [contact the college](/contact).
