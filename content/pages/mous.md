## What our MoUs cover

- **Internships and projects** for students with partner companies.
- **Training and certification** in emerging technologies, including AI and quantum computing.
- **Joint research**, publications and funded projects with faculty and research scholars.
- **Guest lectures, workshops and industry visits** that bring practitioners to campus.
- **Placements** and pre-placement talks for final-year students.
