## What UHV is

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) introduced Universal Human Values (UHV) into the student induction programme and the engineering curriculum. UHV asks a simple question: what does it mean to live and work well with others?

Students explore themselves, their families, society and nature through guided self-reflection and discussion, not through lectures on rules. The aim is clarity: understanding what you value, and acting on it.

## How UHV runs at HKBK

- **Induction (UHV-I)** for every first-year student, before regular classes begin.
- **The UHV course (UHV-II)** as part of the curriculum.
- **Faculty development programmes**, so that mentors can facilitate UHV sessions well.
- **Reflection circles and outreach** through the year.

## Why it matters on an AI First campus

Students here learn to build powerful AI systems. They also need judgement about fairness, privacy and the people their work affects. UHV gives that judgement a foundation, and our values-and-technology talks connect it directly to AI and engineering decisions.
