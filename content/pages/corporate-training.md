## Why train with HKBK

- **Next door to your office.** The campus is directly opposite Manyata Tech Park.
- **Hands-on.** Every programme is built around labs and exercises with your own use cases where possible.
- **Faculty and practitioners together.** Sessions are led by our faculty with guest experts from industry.
- **Flexible.** Run a programme at our campus, at your office or online, on weekdays or weekends.

Participants who complete a programme receive a certificate from HKBK College of Engineering.
