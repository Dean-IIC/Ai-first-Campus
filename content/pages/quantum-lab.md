## Learning tracks

- **Foundations.** Qubits, superposition, entanglement, gates and measurement.
- **Algorithms.** Grover's search, the quantum Fourier transform, and variational algorithms such as VQE and QAOA.
- **Quantum-safe cryptography.** Why quantum computers threaten today's encryption, and how the new NIST standards (ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA) replace it.
- **Research projects.** Final-year and doctoral projects that use quantum computing in chemistry, optimisation or machine learning.

## A quantum-safe campus

Quantum computers powerful enough to break today's public-key encryption do not exist yet. But attackers can store encrypted data now and decrypt it later. Student records and research data stay valuable for decades, so HKBK is moving them to quantum-safe protection early:

- Website traffic uses hybrid post-quantum key exchange where browsers support it.
- Sensitive records are encrypted with keys protected by post-quantum algorithms.
- Digital certificates and transcripts will carry post-quantum signatures that anyone can verify.
- All cryptography is tracked in one inventory, so algorithms can be upgraded quickly as standards evolve.
