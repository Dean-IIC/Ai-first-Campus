## How we prepare students

- **Career readiness training** in aptitude, communication, interviews and group discussions, starting in the early semesters.
- **Industry-ready skills** through the Skill Development Centre, certification courses and AI tools in every programme.
- **Internships** with companies, many of them a short walk away at Manyata Tech Park.
- **Mock interviews and resume reviews** with faculty and alumni mentors.

## For recruiters

Companies can schedule campus drives, internships and pre-placement talks with the placement cell. Contact the college to plan a drive.
