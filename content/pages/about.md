## Who we are

HKBK College of Engineering was founded in 1997 as part of the HKBK Group of Institutions. The college is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.

The campus is in Nagawara, north Bengaluru, directly opposite Manyata Tech Park, one of the city's largest technology business parks. That location shapes how we teach: industry engineers visit as guest faculty, students take internships across the road, and our consultancy and training teams work with companies next door.

We offer the Bachelor of Engineering in five branches (Computer Science, Information Science, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Mechanical, and Electronics & Communication) and doctoral research through six research centres.

The college has committed to becoming an AI First and Quantum Ready campus. Read what that means for [students and staff](/ai-quantum/ai-first), how the [Quantum Lab](/ai-quantum/quantum-lab) works, and follow progress on our [dashboards](/ai-quantum/dashboards).
