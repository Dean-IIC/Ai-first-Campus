HKBK is building an AI-First campus, where artificial intelligence is part of how every student learns, every teacher teaches and every service runs. Students in all five BE programmes learn to use AI tools in their own discipline, to check what those tools produce, and to build AI systems of their own.

Faculty use AI to design richer courses and give faster, more personal feedback, while clear rules keep people, not algorithms, in charge of grades and other decisions that affect students. Leading the change are our AI Faculty Champions, who guide AI adoption in every department, and our AI Student Ambassadors, who run peer workshops and help classmates use AI responsibly.
